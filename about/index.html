RaushanSYNC Maths

Hi, I'm Raushan.

I created this website because I believe mathematics should be understood from first principles - not by memorizing formulas, procedures, or shortcuts. Every mathematical idea has a reason behind it, and my goal is to help you discover those reasons through logic, curiosity, and careful thinking.

Here you'll find carefully designed articles, video lessons, practice problems, and conceptual notes that focus on building genuine understanding. Whether we're studying Algebra, Geometry, Calculus, or Statistics & Probability, I want you to understand not only how to solve a problem, but why the solution works.

This website is designed for curious learners - from students beginning with Class 6 mathematics to anyone exploring more advanced topics. I believe strong mathematical thinking comes from asking questions, recognizing patterns, making logical connections, and understanding ideas deeply rather than memorizing techniques.

I'm currently pursuing a BS in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras, and building RaushanSYNC has become one of my ways of learning. Every lesson I write encourages me to think more carefully, strengthen my own understanding, and explain mathematical ideas as clearly as I can. As this website grows, I hope we can continue learning together.

Thank you for visiting. I hope these lessons inspire you to stay curious, think mathematically, and discover the beauty, elegance, and joy of understanding mathematics one idea at a time.
